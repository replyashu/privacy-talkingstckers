TalkSticker Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

TalkSticker ("the app") turns short voice clips into videos and stickers of cartoon characters talking. This policy explains what happens to your information.

Summary

  • No account or sign-up.
  • Your voice recordings and photos are processed on your phone and are never uploaded to us.
  • We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. The app has no ads.
  • If you allow it (on by default, and you can switch it off in Settings), the app sends anonymous usage statistics and crash reports so we can fix problems.

Microphone and voice

The app records audio only while you hold the record button. Silence trimming, voice effects (such as Female, Robot or speed changes) and lip-sync all run on your device. Your recordings are not sent over the internet. Clips you save stay in the app's private storage on your phone until you delete them.

Photos and photo characters

If you make a character from a selfie or a pet photo, the photo is processed only on your device. Face and mouth positions are found with Google ML Kit's on-device face detection. The cropped photo and mouth position are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. They are never uploaded, and you can delete a photo character at any time. Before you pick a photo, the app asks you to confirm that it shows you or your pet, or that you have permission to use it.

Videos you reply to

If you share a friend's video into TalkSticker to reply to it, the app copies it into its private storage on your phone so it can make your reply video. It is not uploaded to us.

Sharing and sticker packs

When you tap Share, the app creates a video file on your phone and hands it to the app you choose, such as a messaging app. When you add your sticker pack to WhatsApp, WhatsApp reads your sticker images from the app. What happens after that is governed by that app's privacy policy.

Usage statistics and crash reports

If Settings > Privacy > "Share usage and crash reports" is on (the default), the app uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to collect:

  • app interactions, such as which built-in character or voice effect was used, whether a clip was shared, and clip length;
  • crash logs and diagnostics, such as device model, Android version and app version;
  • an app-instance identifier that Firebase generates. This is not your name, phone number or email.

This never includes your voice, photos, captions or the names you give characters. It is processed by Google on our behalf under Firebase's privacy terms. You can turn it off at any time in Settings. To ask us to delete data collected from your device, email the contact address below.

Install source

Links in shared videos point to the app's Google Play page and include which character was used. When the app is installed from such a link, Google Play's Install Referrer tells the app that link's details, so the app can open on the same character. If usage statistics are on, the source is also counted in aggregate.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Please don't make photo characters of children.

Changes

If we change what the app collects, we will update this policy and the app's Google Play data safety section before that version is released.
